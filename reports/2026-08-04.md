## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
### 1) **PLTR** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Quarterly revenue and earnings beat, full-year guidance raised, U.S. commercial revenue up **149% YoY**, U.S. government revenue up **90% YoY** (Motley Fool, Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This is the strongest confirmed momentum setup in the email set; the move is being driven by both growth acceleration and an upward reset in expectations.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** Failure to hold the earnings gap, immediate reversal after the open, or signs the market is selling the news despite the beat.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Premarket price action, opening range, volume, and whether the stock is holding above key intraday support after the open.

### 2) **W** — Breakout / Momentum
- **Catalyst:** Strongest U.S. sales growth and highest free cash flow since 2020; shares jumped more than 15% (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The setup has both fundamental surprise and price expansion, which can support follow-through if institutions keep pressing.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** A failed gap, weak hold above the opening range, or evidence that the move is just short-covering.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Whether the premarket gain can hold, whether volume confirms the move, and whether the chart is reclaiming a clean resistance area.

### 3) **PFE** — Trend Pullback
- **Catalyst:** Beat on profit, raised low end of 2026 revenue guidance (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Not as explosive as **PLTR** or **W**, but the guidance raise can support a steadier re-rating if the stock has been under pressure.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** If price fades the premarket reaction or if the market treats the quarter as too incremental.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Whether there is follow-through beyond the initial pop, and whether the stock can hold the gap instead of reverting.

### 4) **BA** — Reversal Bounce
- **Catalyst:** FAA certification for the 737 Max 7 clears a key delivery hurdle (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Certification removes an uncertainty and may support sentiment around deliveries/cash flow.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** If the stock does not react to the certification, or if the broader market rotates away from the name.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Gap behavior, whether the market has already priced in the news, and whether there is follow-through buying.

### 5) **GRAB** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Revenue up **21.7%**, MTUs hit a record **54M**; stock up around 4% premarket (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** User growth is the cleaner part of the report and can support a continuation move if traders focus on operating momentum.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** If the market zeroes in on the one-time accounting gain behind the net income jump and fades the stock.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Whether the open confirms the premarket bid and whether the move is supported by real price/volume, not just headline enthusiasm.
