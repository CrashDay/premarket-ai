   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Big upside gap plus raised tone can attract continuation buyers if pre-market strength holds.

2. **Arista Networks (ANET)**  
   - **Catalyst:** Expectations-busting Q2 results and first-ever $3B quarterly revenue; AI fabric rollout narrative remains central (The Motley Fool).  
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** AI infrastructure names can trend hard when results reinforce the theme and buyers defend the gap.  
   - **What would invalidate it:** Failure to hold the earnings gap or a quick reversal below the opening range.  
   - **Must check before entry:** Whether the move is being sustained after the open and whether the stock is extended relative to intraday support.  
   - **Actionability:** Good candidate if the gap holds; otherwise it becomes a chase.

3. **Disney (DIS)**  
   - **Catalyst:** EPS beat, parks revenue +10%, streaming revenue +11%, and early trading strength over 4% (The Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).  
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** A positive surprise with improving parks and consumer-products signals can support a multi-day relief move.  
   - **What would invalidate it:** Failure to hold the post-earnings gap or renewed focus on the revenue miss.  
   - **Must check before entry:** Opening location versus pre-market high and whether the stock can reclaim/hold key intraday levels.  
   - **Actionability:** Moderate; good for a bounce, but not as clean as SHOP/ANET.
