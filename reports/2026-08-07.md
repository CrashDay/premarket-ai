## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only names with reasonably clear swing-tade relevance from the source material.

### 1) **ABNB**
- **Catalyst:** Q2 beat on revenue and EPS, raised full-year revenue outlook, and management cited strong demand with first-time bookers up 11% and app bookings at 64% of nights (Motley Fool, Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This is a clean post-earnings momentum setup with both fundamentals and forward guidance improving. The Gen Z / first-time booking narrative gives the market a fresh growth angle.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the gap-up fades and price loses the post-earnings support area, or if the market decides the Gen Z surge is promotional rather than durable.
- **Must check before entry:** Premarket gap size, opening range behavior, volume vs average, and whether the stock can hold gains after the open.

### 2) **NET**
- **Catalyst:** Strong quarter with revenue +36% YoY, EPS beat, and record additions in paying customers, large customers, and developers (Motley Fool, Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Broad customer expansion is usually more durable than one-off deal strength, which can support continuation if buyers keep defending the breakout.
- **What would invalidate it:** A failed gap-and-go, or signs the move is fully priced in after a large premarket jump.
- **Must check before entry:** Whether premarket strength holds above the prior day’s high, and whether the opening volume confirms institutional buying.

### 3) **TWLO**
- **Catalyst:** Fifth straight earnings beat, record revenue of $1.50B, and raised full-year guidance across messaging, voice, and newer AI products (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Repeated beats plus guidance raise can create a multi-day momentum window if the market views demand as stable and expanding.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the gap-up stalls and the stock cannot reclaim/hold intraday VWAP after the open.
- **Must check before entry:** Reaction to the raised guide, whether the stock is extended after the premarket move, and if volume supports continuation.
