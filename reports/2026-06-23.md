## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only the strongest source-backed ideas are listed. These are swing-trade research candidates, not automatic entries.

### 1) **IBM** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst**: OpenAI partnership (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade**: It directly reinforces IBM’s AI enterprise positioning and the stock was already up around 3% pre-open, suggesting market interest.
- **What would invalidate the idea**: If the open fade erases the gap and the market treats the partnership as incremental rather than material.
- **Must check before entry**: Pre-market gap size, first 15–30 minute price action, whether volume confirms the move, and whether the broader tech selloff is overwhelming the story.

### 2) **MU** — Reversal Bounce / Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst**: New agreement with Anthropic plus strategic investment in its funding round (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade**: The memory/storage demand angle is strategically supportive, and the source explicitly notes continued shortage and strong demand.
- **What would invalidate the idea**: If the stock cannot stabilize after the ~8% early decline, or if the market keeps pricing in tech de-risking over the partnership benefit.
- **Must check before entry**: Whether the premarket selloff is overdone, how semis are trading generally, and whether MU can reclaim key intraday levels after the open.

### 3) **CCL** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst**: Earnings before the open; source expects revenue up 6% YoY from higher passenger volume and pricing (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade**: Earnings can be the cleanest near-term catalyst, and the company already had “impressive numbers” last quarter per source.
- **What would invalidate the idea**: Weak guidance, a sell-the-news reaction, or no post-earnings follow-through.
- **Must check before entry**: Actual EPS/revenue versus expectations, guidance commentary, and whether the stock holds the opening range.

### 4) **IBM** and **MU** are the only clear buy-watch names from the provided material.
- I am **not** forcing **QCOM** as a buy candidate because the source emphasizes acquisition talks and a stock drop, but the setup is not yet strong enough to treat as actionable without more confirmation.
