## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
### 1) **NBIS** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** **NVDA** disclosed a **9.3% passive stake** in Nebius (Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This is the clearest pre-market catalyst in the feed and can attract momentum buyers who view it as AI ecosystem validation.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the move fades quickly and price loses the pre-market gap/range support, or if the market treats the stake as a one-off financial investment rather than a strategic partnership.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Liquidity, pre-market hold above opening range, whether volume expands on the open, and whether headlines add any detail beyond the passive stake.

### 2) **AMD** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Launch of its first rack-scale AI system, **Helios**, framed as a challenge to **NVDA** in AI infrastructure (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The AI infrastructure theme is strong pre-market, and this can extend if traders buy the “Nvidia competitor” narrative.
- **What would invalidate it:** If follow-through volume is weak or the market sees the launch as non-differentiated / already priced in.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Opening strength, ability to hold gap gain, any competitor-driven sympathy move across AI hardware names, and whether the move is broad-based or headline-only.

### 3) **GOOGL** — Reversal Bounce / Theme Support
- **Catalyst:** Report that Google is developing a new server chip embedding parts of Gemini AI models into hardware (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Supports the AI capex / custom silicon theme and could draw incremental institutional attention.
- **What would invalidate it:** Lack of confirmation beyond the report, or a gap that stalls without continuation.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Whether the move is holding into the open, if there is real follow-through in volume, and whether traders treat it as strategic or speculative.
