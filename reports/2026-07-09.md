## 5) Ranked Buy Candidates
### 1. **MU** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Reuters says Micron plans to invest up to **$3B** to strengthen the U.S. semiconductor supply chain, and the stock was up **6% premarket** (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** this is a concrete capital-allocation / strategic investment headline that can keep chip sentiment active for more than one session if the market reads it as supportive of long-term demand or domestic positioning.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** if the market treats the spending as margin pressure, if the move fades hard after the open, or if chip semis broadly reverse.
- **Must check before entry:** gap size vs. prior close, whether there is follow-through volume after the open, and whether semis overall are confirming.
- **Pass if:** the gap is extended with no add-back and momentum is already crowded.

### 2. **AZZ** — Reversal Bounce / Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** earnings beat, industrial demand for metal coatings, and full-year outlook raised; shares were up nearly **8% premarket** (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** positive earnings plus raised outlook can support a multi-day reaction if the market believes the industrial demand trend is durable.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** if the move is only a gap-and-go fade, or if price reclaims poorly after the open.
- **Must check before entry:** whether the premarket move is holding, the open relative to premarket high, and whether volume confirms.
- **Pass if:** the gap is too large to chase without a clean intraday base.

### 3. **SKHY** — Breakout / Momentum
- **Catalyst:** Bloomberg reports the U.S. ADR listing is more than **7x oversubscribed** and could raise about **$24.5B** (Motley Fool / Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** very strong demand can create first-day/first-session momentum and keep attention on the name.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** if the offering/ADR trade is already crowded, if momentum stalls after listing, or if the deal details disappoint.
- **Must check before entry:** exact tradability, pricing/structure, and whether the instrument is actually available in your venue.
- **Pass if:** liquidity is unclear or the security is not straightforward to trade.
