## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only names with a reasonably clear catalyst and tradeable setup are included.

### 1) **TGT**
- **Catalyst:** Q1 beat, same-store sales +5.6% YoY, guidance raised to 4% net sales growth.
- **Why it matters for a swing trade:** A first positive comp in five quarters can change near-term sentiment if the market starts pricing a turnaround rather than a one-off beat.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the post-earnings rally fades quickly or guidance is viewed as not enough to sustain re-rating.
- **Check before entry:** How the stock holds after the initial gap; whether volume confirms buyers are defending the move.

### 2) **HAS**
- **Catalyst:** Q1 EPS beat and management said the digital games segment is on track to grow in 2026.
- **Why it matters for a swing trade:** Beaten-down consumer names can get follow-through when a specific growth segment improves the story.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the pre-market pop fails to hold or the market treats the digital-games growth comment as too narrow.
- **Check before entry:** Whether the move is being confirmed in regular trading and whether the beat is being rewarded broadly.

### 3) **GOOG**
- **Catalyst:** Google I/O product launch: major search update, Gemini expansion, new TPU announcements.
- **Why it matters for a swing trade:** Product-cycle headlines can support sentiment if investors view them as strengthening Google’s AI monetization story.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the market decides this is incremental rather than monetarily meaningful.
- **Check before entry:** Whether shares react positively despite broader market weakness and whether AI announcements are being bought, not just discussed.
