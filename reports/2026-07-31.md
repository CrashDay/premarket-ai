## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only ideas supported enough by the source material are included.

### 1) **AMZN**
- **Catalyst:** Quarterly revenue beat, AWS revenue growth of **37%**, and a higher 2026 capex forecast to **$220B** (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The move is aligned with the market’s current preference for companies showing **clear monetization/proof** rather than just spending promises (Motley Fool).
- **What would invalidate it:** If the post-earnings gap fails to hold and price loses momentum after the open, or if traders shift focus from growth proof to margin/capex concerns.
- **Must check before entry:** Premarket high, opening range behavior, whether the gap is holding on volume, and whether broad tech strength persists after the macro data.

### 2) **CVX**
- **Catalyst:** Record U.S. production volumes up **20%** YoY and an agreement to develop a West Texas power facility for Microsoft (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The stock has a clear earnings/operational catalyst and is trading in a market that is willing to reward evidence of scale and execution.
- **What would invalidate it:** If crude/energy sentiment weakens at the open or if the move fades despite the positive report.
- **Must check before entry:** Whether the premarket gain holds, sector tape for energy, and whether the market cares more about the earnings miss/beat mix than the production headline.
  
### 3) **RIVN**
- **Catalyst:** Narrowed quarterly loss, revenue up **27%**, and raised 2026 delivery forecast (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Positive delivery guidance can support a multi-day sentiment move if the market views it as evidence of improving demand.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the stock fails to hold the premarket bounce or if traders dismiss the move as a one-day reaction.
- **Must check before entry:** Whether shares reclaim opening levels, the size of the gap, and whether EV sentiment is supportive.

### 4) **SONY**
- **Catalyst:** Raised full-year operating profit forecast by **8%** after a Q1 beat, driven by gaming and image sensors (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Forecast raises after a beat can extend trend strength if the market likes the mix.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the move is already extended and gives back the premarket reaction.
- **Must check before entry:** U.S. ADR liquidity, opening volume, and whether the gain is holding after the cash open.
