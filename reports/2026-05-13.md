## 3. Earnings And Company News
- **Wix.com** (**WIX**) reported Q1 revenue up **14% YoY** to **$541M** and bookings up **15% YoY**; management still expects **mid-teens** growth in 2026 bookings and revenue. Stock fell **over 10%** early after the update; company repurchased about **30%** of outstanding shares in April (The Motley Fool).
- **PaySign** (**PAYS**) reported record Q1 revenue up **50.8% YoY** to **$28M**, beating the top end of guidance; EPS **$0.09** beat estimates of **$0.07**. Stock was described as showing **no movement** in early trading (The Motley Fool).
- **Alibaba** (**BABA**) reported Q4 revenue below estimates; adjusted net profit fell sharply amid heavy AI spending and competition. Stock was down **3% premarket** in one source; another source said it dipped **2% premarket** (The Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
- **Nebius** (**NBIS**) reported Q1 revenue up **684% YoY** to **$399M** and a smaller-than-expected loss; shares rallied **16% premarket**. The Stock Analysis earnings table also shows a different revenue figure and says revenue missed estimates, so the source set is internally inconsistent on the details (Stock Analysis).
- **Tower Semiconductor** (**TSEM**) forecast Q2 revenue above estimates and announced **$1.3B** in AI-related silicon photonics deals for 2027; shares rallied **17% premarket** (Stock Analysis).
- **Birkenstock** (**BIRK**) missed quarterly sales estimates and cited Middle East conflict impacts on EMEA revenue; shares fell **6% premarket** while full-year guidance was maintained (Stock Analysis).
- **Brown-Forman** (**BF.B**) fell **1%** after rejecting a roughly **$15B** takeover offer from Sazerac; prior talks with Pernod Ricard also collapsed (Stock Analysis).
- **Walmart** (**WMT**) is laying off or relocating about **1,000** corporate employees as it consolidates technology/product teams; shares rose yesterday but were **-1% premarket** (Stock Analysis).
- **Intel** (**INTC**) continues to surge, up **214% since late March** per the source, driven by AI-demand and partnership bets (Stock Analysis).

- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Strong beat-style earnings reactions often extend if institutions chase the AI infrastructure story.
- **What would invalidate it:** The source conflict on the revenue/estimate details, a quick fade after the open, or inability to hold the premarket gap.
- **Must check before entry:** Confirm the actual earnings release numbers, float/liquidity, premarket volume, and whether the gap is being bought or sold.

### 3) **WIX** — Reversal Bounce / Risk Watch
- **Catalyst:** Revenue and bookings growth beat, plus management maintained mid-teens 2026 growth and highlighted AI features.
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** A strong fundamental update can attract dip-buyers if the market decides the AI competition fear is overdone.
- **What would invalidate it:** If sellers keep treating the AI competition narrative as a structural threat and the stock cannot reclaim key intraday levels.
- **Must check before entry:** Whether the selloff stabilizes, whether management commentary changed growth expectations, and whether the buyback support is enough to absorb supply.
