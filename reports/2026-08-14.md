# Pre-Market Briefing

## 1. Market Tone
- **Confirmed:** The email describes a market that is rewarding companies converting spending into profits and punishing those still promising future returns (Motley Fool).

- **Confirmed:** Next week’s calendar includes:

## 3. Earnings And Company News

  - **Confirmed:** This is presented as a major AI-financing/networking development.
- **CAVA Group (CAVA)**: Q2 same-restaurant sales rose 9%, with 5.3 points from guest traffic; stock jumped about 11% after earnings and has kept climbing (Motley Fool).
- **BBB Foods (TBBB)**: Revenue grew 38.7% and same-store sales 20%; shares rose about 7% and have stayed up (Motley Fool).
- **On Holding (ONON)**: Strong growth, but the stock is down nearly 20% after guiding to a slowdown; company will not discount shoes to chase volume (Motley Fool).
- **Wendy’s (WEN)**: Closed 289 domestic restaurants as U.S. same-restaurant sales fell 7% (Motley Fool).
- **Smithfield Foods (SFD)**: Cut its outlook (Motley Fool).
- **Rocket Lab (RKLB)**: Revenue rose 62% YoY and backlog hit a record, but losses and cash burn widened, it missed estimates, and shares fell about 7%; also landed a $397 million Space Force contract and has a Neutron first launch pad target this quarter (Motley Fool).

- **L3Harris Technologies (LHX)**: Market punished it for heavy spending and a delayed public offering of its missile unit (Motley Fool).
- **Intuitive Surgical (ISRG)**: Highlighted as a compounding razor-and-blades model; analyst bought more shares for the first time in 13 years (Motley Fool).
- **Airbnb (ABNB)**: Best quarter in years; revenue up 17% to $3.6 billion, AI now solves 45% of support requests, and stock hit a 52-week high (Motley Fool).
- **Berkshire Hathaway (BRK.B / BRK.A)**: Became a net buyer of stocks for the first time in more than three years; cash pile shrank by about $32 billion and operating earnings rose about 16% YoY (Motley Fool). 13-F filing lands today.

## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
1. **CAVA**
   - **Catalyst:** Q2 same-restaurant sales up 9%, with meaningful guest traffic contribution; stock already reacted positively.
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade:** Strong traffic suggests demand is not purely price-driven, which can support follow-through after earnings.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Any sign that traffic momentum fades, the post-earnings move stalls, or valuation becomes the primary focus instead of operating strength.
   - **What must be checked before entry:** Current price action vs. post-earnings high, whether volume confirms the move, and whether the stock is extended.
2. **ABNB**
   - **Catalyst:** Best quarter in years, revenue up 17%, AI handling 45% of support requests, and shares at a 52-week high.
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade:** New highs with improving efficiency can attract momentum and trend-following buyers.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Failure to hold the breakout area or any evidence the AI benefit is not translating into sustained earnings power.
   - **What must be checked before entry:** Whether price is still near highs, whether the move is overextended, and whether the trial/other external risks are already discounted.
3. **RKLB**
   - **Catalyst:** Record revenue and backlog plus a $397 million Space Force contract and Neutron progress; however, earnings were weak.
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade:** Could work as a catalyst-driven continuation if the contract/roadmap outweighs earnings disappointment.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Ongoing widening losses, worsening cash burn, or the market refocusing on missed estimates.
   - **What must be checked before entry:** Whether the stock is stabilizing after the 7% drop and whether the new contract is enough to reverse sentiment.
4. **BRK.B**
   - **Catalyst:** Ended a three-year buying drought and bought stocks again; 13-F filing is due today.
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade:** The filing may create short-term interest if notable purchases are disclosed.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the 13-F is underwhelming or the disclosed buys do not match market expectations.
   - **What must be checked before entry:** The actual 13-F holdings, whether any disclosed buys are tradeable from a swing perspective, and whether the move is already priced in.
