   - **What would invalidate it:** Any sign the growth pace is not translating into sustained demand or if the broader market rotates out of semis after CPI.
   - **Check before entry:** Price reaction to the July sales print, relative strength versus SOX/QQQ, and whether the stock is extended after the recent sector bounce.
2. **BRK.B**
   - **Catalyst:** Cash pile shrinking as capital is deployed; operating earnings +16%; first net buyer of stocks in over three years.
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Confirms a notable shift in capital allocation and may attract longer-duration buyers.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If market views the buying as insufficient or if the Q2 improvement is dismissed as non-repeating.
   - **Check before entry:** Pre-market price response, whether the stock is near support or already fully repriced, and whether 13-F details on Aug. 14 create better entry timing.
3. **META**
   - **Catalyst:** Clear AI messaging plus launch of a smaller on-device model.
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Reinforces AI narrative in a mega-cap leader and can support momentum if AI stays in favor.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the market treats the essay/model news as non-event or if AI names are sold on CPI concern.
   - **Check before entry:** Whether there is actual price follow-through and if the stock is extended from recent levels.
4. **CELH**
   - **Catalyst:** 4.7% stake disclosed by Rockstar founder with replacement attempt for CEO.
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Activist/strategic-interest setup can create sharp sentiment-driven moves.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the market views the stake/CEO push as noisy, or if no operational plan emerges.
   - **Check before entry:** Whether the stake is purely disclosed or accompanied by a credible turnaround plan; watch for volume and trend confirmation.
5. **RKLB**
   - **Catalyst:** Earnings today after close; company expects record revenue and strong gross margins.
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Could continue a post-earnings momentum move if guidance and execution stay strong.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Any miss on revenue, margins, or capital discipline; spending concerns are explicit in the source.
   - **Check before entry:** Current setup into the print, expectations vs. guidance risk, and whether you are comfortable with earnings risk.
