## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
### 1) **TBBB** — strongest confirmed growth/expansion story
- **Catalyst:** Q2 revenue grew 38.7% YoY to $1.49B, ahead of expectations; 155 net new stores added; total store count reached 3,624, plus a new distribution center (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This is the kind of “expand the footprint and keep comping” setup that can attract momentum buyers if the market believes store growth remains replicable.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** Any evidence the expansion pace is straining finances, slowing store openings, or that the market is no longer rewarding growth over execution.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Premarket hold above prior-day close, volume confirmation, and whether the move is supported by the earnings details rather than just a headline beat.
- **Pass if:** Premarket gap fades quickly or the stock cannot hold early strength.

### 2) **ENR** — profitability-improvement thesis
- **Catalyst:** Beat on revenue and earnings, with strong guidance on profitability (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The article frames the story as margin improvement, which can be rewarded if investors shift from pure growth skepticism to earnings quality.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** If the move is only a relief pop and not backed by follow-through buying or if guidance turns out less durable than the market expects.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Whether the premarket move is being sustained, whether management commentary specifically supports margin expansion, and whether the stock is breaking above nearby resistance.
- **Pass if:** The gap is already extended without continuation volume.

### 3) **AUR** — takeover-proposal speculation, but lower confidence
- **Catalyst:** Curaleaf offered to acquire Aurora Cannabis for about $272M, or $4/share, a more than 45% premium; Aurora is reviewing the unsolicited proposal (StockAnalysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Event-driven upside can persist if traders think a competing bid or negotiated deal is possible.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** If Aurora rejects the offer, if the bid is viewed as non-actionable, or if deal terms weaken.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Confirm the proposal details, jurisdiction/ticker nuances, and whether the stock is trading close enough to the offer to justify risk/reward.
- **Pass if:** The name is already too close to the proposed price or liquidity is poor.

#### **TBBB** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Trigger:** Hold premarket strength and reclaim/hold key intraday support after the open.
- **Stop / invalidation:** Lose premarket support and fail to recover, or show heavy opening rotation down.
- **First target logic:** Prior swing high / next round-number resistance; use the strength of the gap and volume to gauge extension.
- **Sizing caution:** Smaller size if the gap is large; earnings gaps can reverse fast.
- **Pass if:** The open is weak, volume dries up, or the stock fails to hold the earnings gap.

#### **ENR** — Reversal Bounce
