## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
1. **INTC**  
   - **Catalyst:** Reported Apple U.S. chip partnership plus government/onshoring tailwind (Motley Fool, Stock Analysis).  
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This is a fresh narrative catalyst with immediate sentiment impact and potential follow-through if traders start repricing Intel’s foundry optionality.

2. **QURE**  
   - **Catalyst:** FDA appeared to endorse a regulatory pathway for Huntington’s gene therapy, enabling accelerated-approval pursuit (Stock Analysis).  
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Big biotech catalysts can create powerful continuation if volume and news flow remain strong.  
   - **What would invalidate it:** Any clarification that the pathway is weaker than traders assume or that approval probability remains uncertain.  
   - **Check before entry:** Verify the exact FDA language, premarket liquidity/spread, and whether the gap is already extended.

3. **BIRD**  
   - **Catalyst:** Rebrand to AI infrastructure and appointment of a new CEO from AWS (Stock Analysis).  
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Large re-rating moves can persist if the market buys the pivot story.  
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the move is mostly narrative and not backed by a real business shift or execution plan.  
   - **Check before entry:** Confirm float/liquidity, whether the gap holds, and if the AI pivot is more marketing than substance.
