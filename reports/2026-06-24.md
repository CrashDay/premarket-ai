- **Catalyst:** Reported acquisition of **Vibe.co** for **$1.4B** to strengthen Walmart Connect / connected-TV ad capabilities (WSJ via The Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** A strategic ad-tech expansion can support sentiment around higher-margin business mix and revenue diversification.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the market treats the deal as too small to move fundamentals, or if shares fade back and lose the premarket reaction.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Confirm whether the deal is official vs report-only, price/structure details, and how the stock is reacting into the open.

### 2) **QCOM** — catalyst continuation candidate
- **Catalyst:** Agreement to acquire **Modular** for about **$3.9B** in stock; also reported talks for custom chips for ByteDance (Bloomberg / Reuters via Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Reinforces the company’s AI/data-center push and could keep the name in play if investors reward strategic expansion.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the market views the acquisition as expensive or strategically unclear, or if the stock cannot hold gains after the open.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Confirm deal terms, whether the ByteDance talk is just a report, and whether the stock is actually holding relative strength.

### 3) **KBH** — reversal bounce candidate
- **Catalyst:** Revenue beat, with premarket strength despite a reduced full-year outlook tied to affordability issues (The Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** A beat with a maintained or improving bid can support a tactical bounce if housing remains a tradeable theme off the new housing bill.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the lowered outlook dominates the tape or the stock fades back below the opening range.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Open reaction, whether the guidance cut is already fully priced in, and if homebuilder sentiment is improving with today’s housing headlines.

### 4) **FDX** — risk watch, not a clean buy
- **Catalyst:** Beat on revenue/EPS, but guidance and margins disappointed; stock down premarket (The Motley Fool; CNBC via Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Sometimes post-earnings selloffs in quality names create oversold bounces, but this is not strong enough to label as a buy from the email alone.
- **What would invalidate it:** Continued selling after the open, especially if guidance concerns are reiterated by the market.
- **What must be checked before entry:** First-hour price action, whether the gap down is being bought, and if support levels hold.
