# Pre-Market Briefing

## 1. Market Tone
- **Risk-on open tone**: futures are higher, with Dow futures +0.67%, S&P 500 futures +0.54%, and Nasdaq 100 futures +0.65% (CNBC Morning Squawk).
- **Rotation inside tech** is the main story: AI capex and cloud growth are being rewarded, while heavy spending / softer guidance is being punished.
- Recent broader tape has been strong: the S&P 500 closed above 7,200 for the first time ever on Wednesday (Motley Fool).

## 2. Macro And Calendar
**Confirmed**
- The Fed held rates steady yesterday; Powell said the committee had the highest dissent since 1992 (CNBC Morning Squawk; Motley Fool).
- Brent crude surged to **$126 overnight** on Middle East / Iran-related developments (CNBC Morning Squawk).
- PCE data came in as expected at 3.5% YoY, with core PCE at 3.2% YoY, easing inflation worries relative to a surprise scenario (Motley Fool).

**Calendar / near-term items mentioned**

## 3. Earnings And Company News
**Big tech earnings**
- **Alphabet (GOOG)**: beat expectations; cloud revenue jumped 63% YoY and net income rose 81% YoY. Stock up more than 7% pre-market (CNBC; Motley Fool).
- **Amazon (AMZN)**: better-than-expected results; cloud revenue growth was the strongest in more than three years / 28% YoY in the Fool email. Stock up about 3% pre-market (CNBC; Motley Fool).
- **Microsoft (MSFT)**: earnings beat, but Q4 revenue guidance came in below expectations. Stock down about 2% to 3% pre-market (CNBC; Motley Fool).
- **Meta (META)**: shares down 7% to 9% pre-market after headwinds from “internet disruptions in Iran” and a Reality Labs loss of more than $4 billion; also concern about higher capex guidance up to $145 billion for 2026 (CNBC; Motley Fool).

**Other company news**
- **Ford (F)**: beat earnings expectations and raised full-year guidance, citing a $1.3 billion tariff refund benefit; stock mentioned positively in CNBC (no premarket move given).
- **Carvana (CVNA)**: shares up 9% pre-market after record first-quarter results and beats on both revenue and earnings (CNBC).
- **Atlassian (TEAM)**: up 25% pre-market after Q1 revenue rose 32% YoY and non-GAAP EPS rose 80%; AI-powered services demand accelerated (Motley Fool).
- **Reddit (RDDT)**: up over 15% after Q1 revenue rose 69% YoY and results beat expectations (Motley Fool).
- **Twilio (TWLO)**: up over 20% after beating its own Q1 guidance; revenue up 20% YoY, operating income up 31%, and FY revenue growth guide raised to 14%-15% (Motley Fool).
- **Roblox (RBLX)**: down 20% after strong revenue growth but lowered 2026 bookings growth target to 8%-12% from 22%-26% (Motley Fool).
- **Moderna (MRNA)**: up 8% pre-market after Q1 beat, helped by stronger international COVID vaccine sales (Motley Fool).
- **Pershing Square USA (PSUS)**: closed down 18% yesterday, below IPO price (CNBC).

## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Limited to names with enough source support for a swing-trade thesis. No forced calls.

### 1) **GOOG**
- **Catalyst**: Q1 beat with cloud revenue up 63% YoY and net income up 81%; management framed AI investments as paying off (CNBC; Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade**: strong earnings reaction plus clear AI narrative can support follow-through if buyers view capex as productive rather than a drag.
- **What would invalidate it**: a reversal that shows the market is not willing to pay for the capex story, or any fade in the post-gap momentum.
- **Must check before entry**: premarket holding above the gap area, relative strength versus Nasdaq, and whether the open confirms continuation rather than an exhaustion gap.

### 2) **AMZN**
- **Catalyst**: better-than-expected results and cloud revenue growth at the strongest pace in more than three years / 28% YoY (CNBC; Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade**: cloud acceleration is a market-friendly driver and could support multi-day continuation if the opening trade holds.
- **What would invalidate it**: failure to hold gains after the open or any sign the market is more focused on spending than growth.
- **Must check before entry**: whether it opens and holds above the premarket high, volume confirmation, and whether it tracks with other AI/cloud winners.

### 3) **TEAM**
- **Catalyst**: 32% revenue growth and 80% rise in non-GAAP EPS; AI-powered services demand accelerated (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade**: a large gap-up on a strong beat can trend for several sessions if institutions keep chasing the AI software theme.
- **What would invalidate it**: if the stock gives back most of the gap or if management’s comment about more muted data center expansion is interpreted as a growth headwind.
- **Must check before entry**: whether the gap is holding, whether premarket volume is extreme enough to support continuation, and if the open avoids a first-hour fade.

### 4) **TWLO**
- **Catalyst**: beat its own Q1 guidance, revenue up 20% YoY, operating income up 31%, and raised FY revenue growth guidance to 14%-15% (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade**: a beat-and-raise setup with a big premarket gap can attract momentum buyers if the broader software tape is strong.
- **What would invalidate it**: if the market treats the move as a one-day gap fill candidate or if price action fails after the open.
- **Must check before entry**: whether the stock can hold near the premarket extension, plus any sign of support at the opening range.
