## 5) Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only where the source material is strong enough for a swing-trade idea.

### 1. **ZM** — strongest earnings follow-through setup
- **Catalyst:** Clean beat-and-raise, AI Companion license growth **+184% YoY**, adjusted operating margin at **41.1%**, and a **$1.0B** increase in buybacks (The Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This has both fundamental and capital-return support, and after-hours strength suggests momentum buyers may continue to engage.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the post-earnings gap fails to hold, or if the market treats the move as a one-day squeeze and reclaims none of the after-hours gains.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Premarket hold versus after-hours high, opening range behavior, and whether volume confirms continuation.

### 2. **ELF** — earnings + international growth
- **Catalyst:** Beat on revenue and earnings; international sales up **75% YoY** and 29th straight quarter of net sales growth (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** A strong top-line beat plus sustained growth streak can support a multi-day repricing if guidance sentiment is not too negative.
- **What would invalidate it:** If pre-market strength fades and the market focuses on any guidance disappointment rather than growth.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Whether the opening move can hold above premarket high and whether guidance commentary is a headwind.

### 3. **ENS** — data-center power demand tailwind
- **Catalyst:** Strong revenue guidance; demand from data centers needing backup power is being cited as a driver (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This is a clean thematic name tied to AI infrastructure demand, which is getting rewarded across the tape.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the move is purely premarket and fails at the open, or if guidance is not sustained in broader industrial weakness.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Relative strength versus the industrials / power complex and whether the gap is holding.

### 4. **BE** — AI power contract
- **Catalyst:** Nebius deal worth up to **$2.6B** in service fees for Bloom; some capacity expected operational this year (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Large contracted revenue against the AI data-center power shortage narrative is a strong thematic catalyst.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the market considers the move overextended after the earlier surge, or if follow-through is absent.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Gap extension risk, volume, and whether the market still values the Nebius deal as incremental after yesterday’s move.

### 5. **IBM** — quantum funding headline
- **Catalyst:** Included among nine quantum-computing firms receiving a **$2B** White House award with government equity stakes (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Government-backed funding can support a sentiment bid, especially if traders rotate into quantum / advanced compute names.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the market treats the move as headline noise or if the quantum linkage is not viewed as economically meaningful.
- **What must be checked before entry:** Actual premarket liquidity, whether the open holds gains, and whether the market is rewarding the quantum theme broadly or only selectively.
