## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
1. **GOOG**
   - **Catalyst:** Joint AI cloud venture with Blackstone; $5B initial equity investment; TPU-based AI training/inference capacity (The Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** This is a concrete AI-infrastructure expansion narrative, and the market has been rewarding AI-linked capex and compute capacity themes.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the market treats the venture as strategically interesting but financially immaterial, or if the stock fails to hold any premarket/early session strength.
   - **Must check before entry:** Reaction in the first 15–30 minutes, whether volume confirms the move, and whether the broader Nasdaq weakness is overwhelming the trade.

2. **BX**
   - **Catalyst:** Same AI venture; Blackstone is the capital sponsor and already has more than $150B in data-center assets per the Motley Fool source.
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** It adds a visible infrastructure/real-assets AI angle that can attract momentum and institutional interest.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the market sees this as a long-dated asset deployment story with little near-term earnings impact.
   - **Must check before entry:** Whether the stock is reacting in sympathy with GOOG, and whether the move is strong enough relative to the broader market tape.
   - **Risk note:** Likely less direct upside than GOOG if the market wants to trade the AI narrative through cloud/compute exposure.

3. **HD**
   - **Catalyst:** Q1 revenue and EPS beat estimates; guidance was reaffirmed (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** A beat plus guidance support can stabilize a large-cap retail/home-improvement leader, especially with pending home sales and homebuilder sentiment in the background.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the market focuses on weaker large-project demand rather than the headline beat, or if the stock fades after the open.
   - **Must check before entry:** Price reaction versus premarket move, whether the guidance is enough to hold gains, and whether home-related macro data supports the trade.
   - **Risk note:** The source does not show a strong upside surprise beyond the beat, so this is more of a selective swing than a forced buy.
