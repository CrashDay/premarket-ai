## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Limited to ideas with the strongest source support. No forced picks.

### 1) **INTC** — earnings-driven swing candidate
- **Catalyst:** Q2 revenue +25% to $16.1B, EPS beat, gross margin beat, data-center sales +59%, capex raised above $20B (Stock Analysis; The Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The report gives the market multiple reasons to re-rate the stock at the open: demand strength, execution surprise, and a larger investment plan tied to growth.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the open gaps up hard and immediately fades, or if traders decide the capex increase is just spending pressure rather than upside investment. Also invalid if the market rotates away from cyclical/AI-linked names.
- **Must check before entry:** Pre-market price action versus prior close, whether the gap holds after the open, volume confirmation, and whether management commentary supports sustainable demand rather than one-quarter noise.

### 2) **KNSL** — quality earnings plus capital return
- **Catalyst:** Beat on revenue and earnings; announced an additional $250M share repurchase (The Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Buybacks can reinforce the market’s confidence in balance-sheet strength, and a clean beat can support continuation if the stock is already in favor.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the move is too small to show institutional interest, or if the market treats the buyback as routine rather than incremental.
- **Must check before entry:** Relative strength at the open, whether the repurchase announcement is new versus already expected, and whether the stock can hold gains after the first 15–30 minutes.

### 3) **SAP** — earnings strength with a caveat
- **Catalyst:** Cloud revenue rose 24% to $7.1B and backlog grew 26%, despite a cut to annual operating profit outlook (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The market may focus on cloud growth/backlog as the more durable driver, especially if management frames the profit cut as timing-related.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the guidance cut becomes the dominant interpretation, or if the stock can’t sustain premarket strength.
- **Must check before entry:** Price reaction to the opening print, whether the guidance cut is viewed as temporary or structural, and whether the stock is near a key resistance area.
