2. **SNPS**
   - **Catalyst:** Reuters says **SNPS** will stop offering some fab tools and refocus on higher-margin AI offerings (Source 1).

   - **What would invalidate it:** If customers/market interpret the cut as a loss of business rather than a focus shift, or if the stock fails to hold after any opening reaction.
   - **What to check before entry:** Which products are being cut, whether revenue risk is material, and whether price/volume confirm a re-rating.

   - **Catalyst:** Bank ETF at a record high, with major banks due to report soon and expectations for double-digit revenue growth at some names (Source 1).
