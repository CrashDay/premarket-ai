## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> No source here is strong enough to force a high-conviction “buy” list. The best swing candidates are the ones with clear, tradable catalysts and enough follow-through potential.

### 1) **TREX** — Catalyst Continuation / Re-rating candidate
- **Catalyst:** Strong quarterly results, upgraded full-year sales outlook, margin resilience, and a new exclusive North American distributor with broad retail reach (Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The market has a concrete growth/distribution story to price in, and pre-market strength suggests active interest.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the move fades and the stock loses its earnings gap/relative strength, or if investors focus on macro sensitivity to consumer demand instead.
- **Must check before entry:** Open price action vs. pre-market high, volume, whether guidance details are actually better than expected, and whether the move is holding above the gap area.
- **Pass if:** Price fails to hold the pre-market strength and the gap is immediately sold.

### 2) **CLSK** — Breakout / Momentum
- **Catalyst:** 20-year data-center lease expected to generate **$6.6B** in revenue; story shifts it further toward AI infrastructure (Bloomberg via Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Long-duration revenue visibility can attract momentum capital and theme traders.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the market treats the revenue figure as too conditional, or if the stock opens extended and reverses sharply.
- **Must check before entry:** Exact lease terms, whether the move is holding after the open, whether this is a clean breakout or just a news spike.
- **Pass if:** The stock gaps too far above a workable entry and volatility expands without holding support.

### 3) **GS** — Trend Pullback / Financials strength
- **Catalyst:** Beat on earnings and revenue, record equities trading, and stronger dealmaking (Reuters via Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Among banks, GS has the cleanest “quality beat” read-through in the source material.
- **What would invalidate it:** If financials roll over with the broader tape or if the move is already fully priced.
- **Must check before entry:** Relative strength versus **JPM**/**BAC**, intraday hold after the first hour, and whether the gap is supported by volume.
- **Pass if:** It opens weak and loses post-earnings support quickly.
