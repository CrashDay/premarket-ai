- **Catalyst:** Reported Samsung deal worth over **$200B** for AI accelerator-related supply chain support.

### 3) **UAL** — Reversal Bounce / Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Report that United once approached Delta about a merger; both names rose premarket.
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Airline consolidation headlines can spark fast sentiment rerating in the group, especially if buyers chase follow-through.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the move fades and there is no further evidence of actual strategic action.
- **Must check before entry:** Group breadth, whether the stock can hold premarket gains, and whether the move is just headline noise.

### 4) **DAL** — Reversal Bounce / Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Same merger headline as **UAL**.
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Could benefit from sympathy buying if the sector trades on consolidation hopes.
- **What would invalidate it:** Lack of actual deal progression and any failure to hold breakout levels.
- **Must check before entry:** Volume, spread, and whether the stock is acting stronger than the airline ETF/peer group.
