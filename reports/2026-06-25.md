## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
1. **MU**
   - **Catalyst**: Beat/raise quarter, huge data center demand, memory shortages expected to persist, long-term supply agreements (Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade**: This is the cleanest catalyst in the email set and could support follow-through if the gap holds after the open.
   - **What invalidates the idea**: Premarket strength fails to hold, or the stock reverses hard after the macro data / open.
   - **Check before entry**: Opening range, whether the gap is holding above premarket highs, and whether semis remain bid after 8:30 ET data.

2. **QCOM**
   - **Catalyst**: Forecast for $15B in annual data center chip sales by 2029, plus AI deals with **MSFT** and **META** (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade**: A forward-looking AI revenue story can sustain multiple-day momentum if investors buy the long runway.
   - **What invalidates the idea**: If the move is just a morning gap without volume/continuation, or if semis rotate back into **MU** only.
   - **Check before entry**: Premarket extension vs. opening pullback, relative strength versus **NVDA**/**AVGO**, and whether the market treats the announcement as credible long-term growth.

3. **AVGO**
   - **Catalyst**: OpenAI custom chip news positions Broadcom as part of AI infrastructure evolution (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade**: Could benefit if traders view custom silicon as a multi-vendor AI infrastructure theme rather than an **NVDA** replacement story.
   - **What invalidates the idea**: If the market interprets the news as negative for AI semis broadly, or if upside is already fully priced.
   - **Check before entry**: Reaction in **NVDA**, **MU**, and the semiconductor ETF/group; confirm whether the stock holds its gap after the open.

4. **TECH**
   - **Catalyst**: Merck’s $11.4B cash acquisition.
   - **Why it matters for a swing trade**: Takeout news often gives a direct catalyst, though upside may already be near deal terms.
   - **What invalidates the idea**: Deal spread is too tight, or terms/risk aren’t favorable for chasing.
   - **Check before entry**: Exact deal terms, whether the stock is trading near implied acquisition value, and whether there are regulatory/timing concerns in the source material.
