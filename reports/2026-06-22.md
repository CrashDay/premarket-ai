## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
### 1) **APGE** — Catalyst Continuation / Takeout Gap
- **Catalyst:** Reported all-cash acquisition by **ABBV** for **$10.9B** at **$135.11/share** (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Takeout news can support a fast repricing toward the deal terms if the market believes the bid is real and financing/regulatory risk is manageable.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** Any sign the report is inaccurate, the deal is delayed, terms change, or takeover spread widens materially.
- **Must check before entry:** Confirm whether the transaction is officially announced, verify current premarket price versus deal price, and assess headline risk around deal certainty / closing timeline.

### 2) **GETY** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Licensing agreement with **OpenAI** bringing Getty content into ChatGPT search and discovery features; stock up **120% premarket** (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** If the market views this as a real monetization bridge into AI distribution, the stock could see follow-through after the open.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** Fade after the opening spike, disappointing deal economics, or a move that clearly exhausts the gap.
- **Must check before entry:** Confirm current volume, opening range behavior, and whether the move is holding gains after the bell.

### 3) **ACA** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** **CRH** agreed to buy **ACA** for about **$8.5B including debt** (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Deal headlines can create a tradable rerating if the spread remains attractive and the market expects the transaction to close.
- **What would invalidate the idea:** A deal-related reversal, widening spread, or any headline questioning terms/approval.
- **Must check before entry:** Verify the quoted purchase terms and compare live price to implied deal value.
