## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
1. **MU**
   - **Catalyst:** SK Hynix’s U.S. listing may reinforce investor focus on AI memory demand and the valuation backdrop for memory peers; Micron also has its own **$9.3B** Hiroshima cleanroom expansion news (Motley Fool).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The stock is already seeing a pre-market reaction and sits in a favorable thematic lane if the AI-memory narrative stays hot.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the open fades and the sector loses momentum, or if the move is purely sympathy without follow-through.
   - **Check before entry:** Pre-open strength, semis breadth, whether **MU** is holding relative strength versus the SOX/AI complex, and whether the move is being sold into.

2. **SKHY**
   - **Catalyst:** Launch of a **$28B Nasdaq ADR offering/listing** to tap U.S. investors and AI-memory demand (Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Fresh listing/expansion can attract attention from U.S. investors and create a short-term scarcity/novelty bid.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Weak reception to the offering, limited tradability/liquidity, or if the IPO/listing hype fails after the open.
   - **Check before entry:** Actual listing mechanics, float/liquidity, tradable instrument details, and whether the price action is orderly or erratic.

3. **EZJ**
   - **Catalyst:** Raised takeover offer at **690 pence/share** and board support in principle (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Deal premium and deadline extension can support bid-like price action.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Any sign the deal stalls, financing terms change, or the spread narrows sharply after the initial jump.
   - **Check before entry:** Finality of the “in principle” wording, deal terms, and whether the stock is still near the offer spread.

4. **RIVN**
   - **Catalyst:** Raised 2026 delivery guidance after topping Q2 delivery expectations (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Guidance raise can sustain momentum if the market views it as an execution inflection.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the move is already fully priced in or delivery strength doesn’t translate into margin/quality confidence.
   - **Check before entry:** Gap size, whether there is follow-through volume, and whether the market is rewarding the guidance raise.
