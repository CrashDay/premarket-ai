## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> No high-conviction buys are obvious from the source set. Below are the **best swing-trade candidates to research**, not automatic buys.

### 1) **SYNA** — potential takeover-style swing
- **Catalyst:** all-stock acquisition by **ON** valued at **$7B** (Stock Analysis / WSJ).
- **Why it might matter:** targets in announced deals often trade toward deal value, but upside depends on terms, spread, and deal confidence.
- **What invalidates it:** deal terms changing, financing/approval issues, or spread narrowing after an initial pop.
- **Check before entry:** exchange ratio, implied deal value vs current price, regulatory/closing risk, and whether the move already reflects the announcement.

### 2) **BB** — post-earnings momentum continuation
- **Catalyst:** raised fiscal 2027 revenue guidance after strong Q1 results; revenue up 26% and beat estimates (Stock Analysis / WSJ).
- **Why it might matter:** guidance raises can support follow-through if the market views embedded software / AI optionality as durable.
- **What invalidates it:** gap-and-fade after the open, analyst pushback, or price failing to hold post-earnings support.
- **Check before entry:** premarket extension, volume confirmation, and whether the gap is above key resistance or already overextended.

### 3) **TII** — policy-driven mineral theme momentum
- **Catalyst:** U.S. Army lease plan for critical-mineral processing plants; shares surged 25% premarket (Stock Analysis / WSJ).
- **Why it might matter:** policy and supply-chain themes can sustain multi-day momentum if the market starts repricing domestic critical-mineral exposure.
- **What invalidates it:** if the move is purely headline-driven and fades quickly, or if there is no clear follow-through in related names.
- **Check before entry:** liquidity, float/borrow conditions, and whether the news actually benefits the company directly versus the broader theme.
