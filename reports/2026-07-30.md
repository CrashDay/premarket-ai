## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
1. **MSFT**  
   - **Catalyst:** Earnings beat, Azure annual revenue above $100B, Copilot paid users at 30M, and strong cash generation narrative (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The reaction is strongly supported by both fundamental beat and a “can fund AI build-out” thesis, which can sustain follow-through if the market keeps rewarding profitable AI spend.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Any sign the rally is purely gap-driven and fades, or if later commentary undercuts cash flow durability / AI monetization.
   - **Check before entry:** Pre-market hold near highs, opening range behavior, and whether the broader tape supports risk-on follow-through.

2. **FTNT**  
   - **Catalyst:** Results exceeded prior guidance for revenue and earnings; full-year revenue guidance was raised (The Motley Fool).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Raising guidance after a strong beat often supports multi-day momentum if the market views the growth as durable.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the gap-up fails to hold or if traders decide the move already prices in the guide raise.
   - **Check before entry:** Whether the stock can hold its pre-market strength and whether volume confirms institutional buying.

3. **SBUX**  
   - **Catalyst:** Strong results, raised full-year earnings guidance, and nearly 6% same-store sales growth tied to the “Back to Starbucks” turnaround (The Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Turnaround narratives can extend if the quarter is seen as proof of operating traction.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the market treats the move as a one-quarter relief rally instead of a sustained operational improvement.
   - **Check before entry:** Gap retention, reaction to the open, and whether management’s guidance language suggests follow-through beyond one quarter.

4. **CBZ**  
   - **Catalyst:** Take-private cash deal at $55/share (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** M&A names often trade tightly around deal value if closing risk seems low.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Any spread widening or uncertainty about deal completion.
   - **Check before entry:** Compare current price vs. deal value and verify deal terms / closing timeline.
