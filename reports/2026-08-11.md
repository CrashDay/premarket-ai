## 5) Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only ideas with enough source support are included. These are research candidates, not signals.

### 1. **CRWD** — Cybersecurity leader riding AI-threat spending
- **Catalyst:** Cybersecurity stocks rallied after Black Hat as AI agents increase attack surface; **CRWD** and **PANW** both hit fresh highs (The Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The move is aligned with a clear theme and already showing relative strength; momentum can extend when a sector re-rates on a new threat narrative.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the AI-threat/cyber spending theme fades, or if the stock loses breakout momentum after the fresh-high move.
- **Must check before entry:** Intraday hold above prior high/breakout area, volume confirmation, and whether the move is sector-wide or just headline-driven.

### 2. **PANW** — Same AI-security theme, potentially cleaner relative strength
- **Catalyst:** Same AI-threat-driven cybersecurity rally; shares jumped more than 5% to fresh highs (The Motley Fool).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Leadership names in strong themes can trend for several sessions if institutional buying persists.
- **What would invalidate it:** Rejection back into prior range or failure to hold the new highs.
- **Must check before entry:** Gap extension risk, whether it opens too extended, and if the stock can base rather than immediately fade.

### 3. **ACHR** — New strategic tie-up with Boeing
- **Catalyst:** Boeing is taking a 19.75% stake in Archer through an asset transaction involving Wisk Aero, SkyGrid and Insitu (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Strategic aerospace validation can attract momentum traders and sympathy interest if the market views the deal as credibility-enhancing.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the stock gives back the opening move or the market decides the deal is more complex than bullish.
- **Must check before entry:** Size of the gap, premarket volume, and whether the market interprets the transaction as net positive after dilution/structure considerations.

### 4. **VREX** — Acquisition arbitrage / deal premium
- **Catalyst:** Teledyne agreed to acquire Varex for about $18.90/share in cash; shares were up about 50% premarket (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Deal premiums can remain firm if no financing/regulatory concerns emerge.
- **What would invalidate it:** Spread widening, deal uncertainty, or a competing interpretation of the transaction.
- **Must check before entry:** Current price vs. deal price, spread, merger terms, and whether there is any announced risk to closing.

### 5. **INTC** — Capital raise tied to AI investment
- **Catalyst:** Intel increased its stock sale to $20B at $95/share to fund capex and working capital as AI demand accelerates (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The AI capex story can support strategic repositioning, but this is a mixed catalyst because dilution is also present.
- **What would invalidate it:** If the market focuses on dilution and balance-sheet strain instead of AI investment.
- **Must check before entry:** How shares trade relative to the offering price, whether the stock is absorbing the news, and any updated company commentary.
- **Sizing caution:** Keep size modest; this is more event-driven than cleanly bullish.
