## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only ideas with enough source support are listed.

1. **BABA**  
   - **Catalyst:** The company reportedly banned Anthropic AI tools internally and is pushing its own assistant; shares were up 9% premarket (Stock Analysis).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Clear headline momentum plus a strong premarket gap can create follow-through if traders treat this as an AI / China software control story.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the move fades quickly after the open or the story is treated as routine corporate policy rather than a material AI monetization signal.
   - **Check before entry:** Premarket gap size vs. prior resistance, first 15–30 minute volume, whether the opening range holds, and if the news is being interpreted as positive product adoption rather than just internal compliance.

2. **NVDA**  
   - **Catalyst:** No direct upside catalyst in the source; however, one source notes it has lost about $1T in market cap since May and is at its cheapest valuation since before the AI boom, while another cites a large chip customer still using Nvidia’s new CPUs and projecting strong sales for those chips (Stock Analysis; Motley Fool).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** If the broader semis group stabilizes after the pullback, NVDA could become a mean-reversion / value-snapshot trade.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Continued sector de-risking, more competitive AI-chip headlines, or failure to reclaim intraday support.
   - **Check before entry:** Whether semis are still selling off at the open, how NVDA reacts to the index weakness, and whether this is only a valuation narrative without actual bid support.
   - **Note:** This is a weaker candidate than **BABA** because the source gives more of a context story than a fresh bullish catalyst.

3. **AAPL**  
   - **Catalyst:** Apple is testing DRAM chips from ChangXin Memory and reportedly committed more than $30B to Broadcom in a multiyear U.S. chipmaking deal, but it also lost an EU appeal over App Store / iOS rules (Stock Analysis; Motley Fool).
   - **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** The Broadcom deal could be viewed as supportive for supply-chain / AI-infrastructure expectations, though the legal headline adds offsetting noise.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the EU ruling becomes the dominant narrative or if the chip-supplier partnership is not read as earnings-relevant.
   - **Check before entry:** Which headline the market is prioritizing, whether **AVGO** strength confirms the chip narrative, and whether **AAPL** is holding premarket gains or fading.
