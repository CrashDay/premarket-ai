## 3. Earnings And Company News
### Confirmed company updates
- **Shopify**: Q2 revenue grew **33.7% YoY**, operating profit rose **68%**, free cash flow rose **55%**, and GMV hit **$115.6B**. Stock closed up roughly **17%** yesterday. Management guided Q3 free cash flow margins to the **high-teens to low-twenties** (Motley Fool).
- **Figma**: stock fell more than **15% pre-market** after R&D more than doubled and total operating expenses hit **$426.9M**, nearly double a year ago. Commentary frames this as an AI spending concern, not a proof that the investment is failing (Motley Fool).
- **MercadoLibre**: revenue and financial income rose **50% YoY**, but margins shrank due to heavier spending. Stock fell about **4%** (Motley Fool).
- **HubSpot**: down about **20%** after guidance implied about **14% revenue growth**, below prior low-20s expectations (Motley Fool).
- **Paycom**: up more than **14%** after adjusted EBITDA margin reached **44.2%**, beat estimates, and management lifted full-year guidance (Motley Fool).
- **Duolingo**: down more than **10%**; revenue rose **18.3%**, but bookings grew only **8%** and a one-time “Streak Revival” event inflated user growth (Motley Fool).
- **Nintendo**: beat on revenue and profit, but Switch 2 hardware sales fell **34.4% YoY**; game sales and the Super Mario Galaxy movie drove results (Motley Fool).
- **Celsius**: down more than **10% pre-market** despite revenue near **$818M**; gross margin fell to **48.1%** from **51.5%** and adjusted EPS dropped **23%** (Motley Fool).
- **The Trade Desk**: earnings are due after the close; revenue growth is expected to keep slowing, but no numbers were provided (Motley Fool).

### 2) **PAYC**
- **Catalyst:** adjusted EBITDA margin of 44.2% beat expectations; management raised full-year guidance.
- **Why it matters for a swing trade:** beat-and-raise plus margin strength often attracts multi-day follow-through when the market wants quality earnings.
- **What would invalidate it:** failure to hold the earnings gap or any sign the market views the guide raise as already priced in.
- **Check before entry:** size of the gap, premarket liquidity, and whether the stock is holding above key intraday support.
- **Pass if:** the open is extended and volume fades quickly.

### 3) **TTD**

- **Why it matters for a swing trade:** this is an event-driven setup, but it is not a premarket buy yet because the catalyst is not confirmed.
- **What would invalidate it:** disappointing earnings or guidance, or a confirmed structural slowdown.
- **Check before entry:** post-close reaction, guide, and whether management addresses the expense reset clearly.
