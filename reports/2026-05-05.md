   - **Catalyst:** Fastest sales growth since Q2 2024, 11% MAU growth, and guidance above expectations (Source 2).
   - **Why it may matter for a swing trade:** User-growth plus monetization narrative can support a multi-day momentum move if the market keeps rewarding ad-platform recovery.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Weak follow-through after the gap or evidence that the monetization story is not being rewarded.

3. **DIS**
   - **Catalyst:** Revenue beat, EPS beat, raised buybacks, and double-digit adjusted EPS growth forecast for fiscal 2027 (Source 3).
   - **Why it may matter for a swing trade:** Multiple positive levers reduce reliance on a single catalyst.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the market fades the beat and the stock cannot hold premarket gains.
   - **Must check before entry:** Whether the move is extending beyond the initial gap and if buyback/news flow is enough to sustain trend.

4. **NVO**
   - **Catalyst:** Strong Q1 profit and Wegovy pill sales; guidance slightly raised (Source 3; Source 4).
   - **Why it may matter for a swing trade:** Continued weight-loss franchise momentum can support follow-through if investors stay focused on the pill opportunity.
   - **What would invalidate it:** Any reversal in sentiment around the pill opportunity or failure to maintain premarket strength.
   - **Must check before entry:** Open reaction, sector sympathy in pharma/obesity names, and whether the guidance raise is enough to hold trend.

5. **INTC**
   - **Catalyst:** Apple reportedly exploring Intel as a chip manufacturer to reduce supply-chain risk and diversify away from TSMC (Source 2; Source 4).
   - **Why it may matter for a swing trade:** Strategic customer speculation can drive fast sentiment-based momentum.
   - **What would invalidate it:** If the Apple tie-up remains only speculative and premarket strength fades.
   - **Must check before entry:** Whether the move is purely headline-driven, confirmation of sustained volume, and any reversal after the open.
