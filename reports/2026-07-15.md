## 5. Ranked Buy Candidates
> Only names with comparatively durable, trader-relevant catalysts are included. This is not a recommendation.

### 1) **ASML** — Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Strong quarterly earnings and second guidance raise this year; demand tied to AI-driven chip capacity expansion (Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Earnings surprise plus guidance revision can support follow-through if semis stay in favor and buyers treat the report as confirmation of a multi-quarter growth trend.
- **What would invalidate it:** Price fails to hold the post-earnings gap, or the market decides the move was fully priced in.
- **What to check before entry:** Where it holds relative to the opening range, whether the gap is being defended, and if semis broadly confirm.
- **Pass if:** The stock cannot reclaim key premarket highs or the tape weakens after the open.

### 2) **PYPL** — Reversal Bounce / Catalyst Continuation
- **Catalyst:** Confirmed takeover bid from Stripe and Advent at **$60.50/share**, about a **28% premium** (Motley Fool; Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Deal headlines can create sharp repricing and short-covering; if the bid is viewed as credible, the stock may hold up as a special situation.
- **What would invalidate it:** Deal uncertainty, financing/completion concerns, or a gap-fade that loses most of the premium.
- **What to check before entry:** Whether the stock is trading near the indicated offer level, how much of the premarket gain is retained, and any read-through on deal certainty.
- **Pass if:** The stock is extended far above the bid without confirmation or liquidity is too thin for a clean entry.

### 3) **TSEM** — Breakout / Momentum
- **Catalyst:** $3B expansion in Japan backed by a $1B government grant, plus higher 2028 revenue/profit targets (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** New capacity plus target raises can attract momentum buyers if the market views this as a meaningful step-up in earnings power.
- **What would invalidate it:** The move stalls and fades because the headline is seen as long-dated or already priced.
- **What to check before entry:** Whether the breakout is holding, volume confirms the move, and the market believes the grant/expansion meaningfully changes the growth path.
- **Pass if:** It gaps too far above prior resistance without follow-through.

### 4) **BLK** — Trend Pullback / Earnings Momentum
- **Catalyst:** Better-than-expected Q2 results and record **$15.34T** AUM, aided by strong ETF inflows and rising markets (Stock Analysis).
- **Why it might matter for a swing trade:** Strong results from a large asset manager can support a multi-day institutional bid if market sentiment stays risk-on.
- **What would invalidate it:** A failed breakout or a quick reversal that shows traders are selling the beat.
- **What to check before entry:** Whether premarket strength persists after the open, and whether financials hold up broadly.
- **Pass if:** The stock is already stretched and offers poor risk/reward into resistance.
